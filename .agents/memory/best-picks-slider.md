---
name: Best Picks slider
description: Display and motion rules for the homepage Best Picks product carousel.
---

The homepage Best Picks carousel shows four cards in a clipped 2×2 window. Its larger verified-product pool must scroll upward continuously in a seamless duplicated loop, rather than stopping on a timer or rendering the full track.

**Why:** The desired browsing treatment keeps the section focused while still surfacing additional products without a manual page-length grid.

**How to apply:** Keep two cards per row and two rows in the viewport, feed the slider more than four products, and preserve its linear upward motion. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep manual next/previous controls usable.
