---
name: Image-first catalogue browsing
description: Browsing behavior for categories where only some current catalogue SKUs have verified photography.
---

Default catalogue browsing should prioritize products with a verified mapped image, without excluding products that do not have one. Keep a clear Latest Arrivals choice for shoppers who prefer chronological ordering.

**Why:** External catalogue updates can introduce new SKUs before their product photos are available. Leading with placeholders makes category pages look incomplete even when a substantial verified image set exists.

**How to apply:** Keep image matching strict and preserve every product in the catalogue. Use image-first ordering only as the visual default; do not invent representative product images or hide unmatched SKUs.
