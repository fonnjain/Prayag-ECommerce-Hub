---
name: Hero media hierarchy
description: Placement rule for PRAYAG's rotating hero videos and featured product content.
---

Hero product reels belong in the full-bleed homepage hero background. The featured panel should show a verified CP Faucet product image and its product details, not a second video or poster carousel.

**Why:** This preserves the intended immersive hero treatment while keeping the featured card readable and visually calm.

**How to apply:** Keep the active carousel video's WebM-first source in the hero background layer; use the photo-ready featured faucet product for the separate product card, with a CMS fallback when the API product is unavailable.
