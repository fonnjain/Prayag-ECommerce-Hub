---
name: Hero media hierarchy
description: Placement rule for PRAYAG's rotating hero videos and featured-series content.
---

Hero product reels belong in the full-bleed homepage hero background. The featured-series panel should show the corresponding static poster image, not another video player.

**Why:** This preserves the intended immersive hero treatment while keeping the featured card readable and visually calm.

**How to apply:** Keep the active carousel video's WebM-first source in the hero background layer; sync the featured card's title, copy, controls, and poster image to the same carousel item.
