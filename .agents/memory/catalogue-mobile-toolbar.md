---
name: Catalogue mobile toolbar
description: Responsive rules for the catalogue filter, sort, and view controls.
---

On narrow catalogue screens, let the filter trigger and sort/view controls wrap rather than requiring them to share one row.

**Why:** The controls can individually fit while their combined intrinsic width exceeds the content column, causing a page-level horizontal scrollbar that is unrelated to the product cards.

**How to apply:** Verify the catalogue page at about 390px after changing toolbar text, sorting, or view controls. Keep the sort control compact and preserve the visible filter and view-toggle actions when the toolbar wraps.
